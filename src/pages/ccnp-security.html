Questions

Is this CCNP Security material really free?

Yes. The notes, flashcards and practice exam are free to use with no account. Flashcard progress is saved in your own browser, and nothing about you is sent anywhere.

Is it updated for SCOR 350-701 v2.0?

Yes. Everything here follows the v2.0 exam topics that Cisco has tested since 27 August 2026. The v2.0 changes guide lists everything that moved from v1.1.

Are these real exam questions?

No. Every question is written from the public exam topics and Cisco documentation. Real exam questions (dumps) break Cisco's candidate agreement and can cost you your certification.

What's the best way to use these tools?

Read a lecture, then do its flashcards the same day and again when they come due. Once a domain feels solid, take the mock exam in practice mode, then in timed exam mode. Use the per-domain score to choose what to reread.

How is the exam weighted?

Network Security 25%, Security Concepts 20%, Cloud Security 15%, Endpoint Protection and Detection 15%, Network Access, Visibility, and Enforcement 15%, and Secure Service Edge 10%. The mock exam picks questions in the same proportions.

About this material

Independent study notes, not affiliated with or endorsed by Cisco. CCNP, CCIE and SCOR are Cisco trademarks. Every question here is written from the public exam topics and product documentation. There are no exam dumps on this site and there never will be: they break Cisco's candidate agreement and teach you nothing.
