The 6 steps (CompTIA A+)

Network+ uses 7 steps: step 4 is split into "plan" and "implement or escalate". If a theory is wrong, loop back. If two theories fail or the problem is outside your access, escalate with your notes.

#StepWhat you do
1Identify the problemAsk the user, find what changed, reproduce it
2Establish a theoryQuestion the obvious; research the KB and vendor docs
3Test the theoryConfirmed? Plan the fix. Not? Form a new theory
4Plan and implementCheck side effects, back up, then apply the fix
5Verify and preventThe user confirms it works; stop it recurring
6DocumentFindings, actions, outcome and lessons learned

Step 1: ask the user

  • When did it start? Did it ever work?
  • What changed: an update, a new device, a moved desk?
  • What is the exact error text? Can you send a screenshot?
  • Is it just you, or others too? Do other devices have it?
  • Can you show me the steps?

Step 2: question the obvious

  • Power, cable, Wi-Fi switched on?
  • Restarted (really)?
  • Updates pending?
  • Caps Lock or keyboard layout?
  • Right account or tenant?
  • Disk or mailbox full?
  • Date and time correct?
  • Service outage page?

Ways to find the cause

MethodHow
Bottom-upOSI L1 to L7: cable, link, IP, app
Top-downStart at the app when the network looks fine
Divide and conquerStart in the middle: ping the gateway
CompareDiff against a working device or user
One changeChange one thing, test, then the next

Caution. Back up user data before reimaging, resetting profiles or replacing disks.

Danger. Suspect malware or a breach? Stop. Disconnect from the network, do not wipe, and escalate to security. Verify identity before any password reset.

Escalate with

  • Impact: who and how many
  • Steps to reproduce
  • What you tried and the result
  • Logs and screenshots
  • Priority and the SLA clock
  • Best contact time

Talk to the user

  • Say what you will do next and when you will update them
  • Skip jargon; repeat the issue back in their words
  • Never blame the user; thank them for the details

Remember

  • You can't prove a fix if you can't reproduce the problem
  • Escalate early, with what you tried and the evidence
  • Tell the user what you are doing and give an ETA
  • Close the ticket only after the user confirms
