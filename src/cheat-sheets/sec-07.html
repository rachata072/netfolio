What changed from 2021

  • Expanded: A03 Software Supply Chain Failures grew from 2021's Vulnerable and Outdated Components.
  • New: A10 Mishandling of Exceptional Conditions.
  • Merged: SSRF now sits inside A01.
  • Moved up: Security Misconfiguration went from #5 to #2.

Five habits that cover most

  • Server-side checks: never trust the browser
  • Parameterize: every query, every time
  • Patch dependencies: scan and update weekly
  • MFA: for users and admins
  • Log and alert: logins, access denied, errors

Test safely

Authorized use only. Test only apps you own or have written permission to test. Use practice targets like OWASP Juice Shop.

Caution. Scanners can change or delete data. Point them at a test copy, never production.
