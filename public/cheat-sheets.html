How every sheet is made

  • Checked twice. Every fact is confirmed in two sources, one of them official: RFCs, vendor docs, NIST, OWASP or exam objectives.
  • Safe examples. Documentation-only addresses (192.0.2.0/24, 2001:db8::/32, example.com), placeholder secrets, and a caution or danger flag on anything that changes or breaks a device.
  • Clean files. PDFs are scanned before publishing: no scripts, forms, attachments or outside links. Each download lists its SHA-256 hash so you can verify it.
  • Free to share. CC BY-NC 4.0: print it, share it with your class, keep the credit line.
