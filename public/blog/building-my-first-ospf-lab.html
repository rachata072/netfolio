The topology

Three areas hanging off a backbone: Area 0 in the middle, a standard area, and a stub. Five routers total, with R1 and R2 acting as ABRs.

Verification commands I lived in

R1# show ip ospf neighbor
R1# show ip route ospf
R1# show ip ospf database
R1# show ip protocols

What actually confused me

  • Type 3 LSAs are generated by the ABR, not flooded unchanged between areas.
  • A mismatched hello timer silently kills adjacency - check it first.
  • passive-interface still advertises the network; it just stops hellos.
