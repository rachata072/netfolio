Packet Tracer labs

Free hands-on labs for SCOR 350-701 v2.0. Each one gives you a network that works but was built the lazy way. You download it, harden it step by step, and prove every control with the same show commands you'd use on the job.

Available labs (1)

  1. PTSegmentation & Layer 2 Hardening LabDownload a working but insecure switched network and harden it step by step: management baseline, VLANs, trunks, port security, BPDU Guard, protected ports and unused-port lockdown.2.360 to 90 min · download .pkt + .pka

How every lab works

  • Read the notes first. Each lab belongs to a lecture that explains the attacks and why each defense exists.
  • Download the baseline. A .pkt file for Cisco Packet Tracer (free with a Cisco Networking Academy account). Some labs add a graded .pka activity. Every file lists its SHA-256 hash.
  • Do, See, Why, Checkpoint. Every step tells you what to type, what output to expect, which attack it stops, and how to prove it worked.
  • Answer configs are hidden until you open them, so try it yourself first.

What Packet Tracer can't do

Packet Tracer simulates IOS, so a few real-IOS commands, attack tools like Yersinia and macof, and products such as FTD, ISE and Secure Access aren't available. Each lab lists what's missing and how to practice it in Cisco Modeling Labs or on real gear.
