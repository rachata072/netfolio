Privacy

%SYS-6-PRIVACY/Last updated Sep 25, 2026

This is a personal portfolio, not a business collecting your data, so this page is short on purpose. Here's exactly what happens when you visit.

What's actually collected

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to see aggregate numbers like page views, visit counts, referring sites, and page load performance. It does not use cookies, does not set any persistent identifier in your browser, and does not track you across other sites. Cloudflare's own documentation describes it as privacy-first for exactly that reason: there's nothing stored client-side, and nothing that lets this site or Cloudflare recognize you individually from one visit to the next.

The email link in the About section is a plain mailto: link. Clicking it opens your own email client; nothing is submitted to this site or stored anywhere by it.

What isn't collected

There's no account system, no comment section, no newsletter sign-up, no contact form, and no third-party advertising of any kind. Nothing here is sold, shared, or used to build a profile of you. If a future project on this site (a lab tool, a calculator) ever asks for input, that input stays in your browser and is never sent anywhere unless the page explicitly says otherwise.

The hosting layer

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare's infrastructure necessarily sees the IP address and request metadata of every visit as part of normal request handling, and retains standard operational logs under its own policies rather than mine. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for how that layer works. This is standard for any site on the internet, not something specific to what's built here.

Fonts and scripts

Every font, stylesheet and script on this site is served from this domain. There are no Google Fonts, no CDNs, and no third-party embeds, so loading a page does not send your IP address to anyone other than the host described above.

Questions

If you have a question about any of this, reach out at treyrct858@gmail.com. This page may be updated as the site changes; the date above reflects the last update.
